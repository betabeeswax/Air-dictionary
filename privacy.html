Privacy Policy

AIR Dictionary (AI, Really Dictionary) · Last updated: September 17, 2026

AIR Dictionary was MADE WITH AI help and uses real open dictionary and thesaurus data.

Disclaimer: Synonyms and antonyms come mainly from open thesaurus data (WordNet). Other dictionaries may list different words for the same entry. Sources do not always agree.

1. What this app is

AIR Dictionary is a free word lookup tool. It shows definitions, example sentences, synonyms, antonyms, linked words, word equations (break-up), and simple real-life / subject notes. You can use it online or download the HTML file for offline use.

2. Made with AI

This app was built with AI assistance. Word meanings and thesaurus pairs are not invented as fake definitions: they come from real linguistic datasets listed below. Some display text (layout, fallback sentences when no example exists, and a small number of cleaned synonym lists) was prepared with AI help.

3. Data sources

  • Wordset — open dictionary definitions and examples
  • Princeton WordNet — open thesaurus (synonyms and antonyms)
  • A small set of hand-checked lists for words that mixed senses badly in open data

That content is language data, not personal information about you.

4. Information we collect

  • No account required. We do not ask for your name, email, or password.
  • Lookups: Word searches run in your browser from data inside the page. There is no login database operated by this app.
  • Hosting: If the site is hosted (for example on Netlify), the host may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit under the host’s own policy.
  • Outbound links: Links to Google, Dictionary.com, or other sites are outside our control. Their privacy policies apply when you leave this page.

5. Ads

This version is not set up for ads. If ads are added later, this policy should be updated.

6. Cookies

This app does not use cookies for accounts. A host platform might use cookies for security or traffic. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

7. Children

This is a general education-style tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through forms on this app (there are no signup forms).

8. Download / offline

You may save the HTML file and use it offline. Offline use stays on your device.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when it does.

10. Contact

For questions, contact the person who published the site link (the Netlify or site owner).
